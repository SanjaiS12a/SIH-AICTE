# ACSS permission, privacy and annotation handoff

This packet is preparation, not a privacy certificate or an evaluated dataset. Keep it in the restricted `data/research/` workspace. It is not imported into the portal, uploaded to an external service, trained on or model-scored.

The publisher's current released Dataverse metadata for [10.7910/DVN/EITGBB](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/EITGBB) specifies **CC BY-NC 4.0**. The associated article describes CC BY 4.0. Use the narrower current publisher conditions for this intake; public availability is not clearance for redistribution, commercial use or incident-level training. A clarification from the publisher is still needed to settle the historical discrepancy. No permission request has been sent on the owner's behalf.

`tests/research/acss_packet.py` verifies the inspected workbook SHA-256, selects 20 bounded unique literal Complaint-labelled narratives in source order, excludes formula/missing narratives and reuses the existing conservative masking. This is a convenience sample, not a representative education test set. The ten adjacent, unscored pairs are reviewer scaffolding: adjacency does not establish a common institution, incident or retrieval eligibility. Automatic masks can miss names and identifying context.

The restricted packet contains source-row provenance, masked text, a privacy/identity review CSV, two independently shuffled blank reviewer CSVs, blank adjudication and a manifest explicitly denying evaluation/training/redistribution clearance. Original workbook and record hashes remain private. Nothing supplies invented Permanent IDs or incident labels.

Complete these gates in order:

1. **Permission:** the project owner records the applicable non-commercial research purpose, attribution, access control, retention/deletion date, redistribution restrictions and publisher clarification. Record separate approval for any proposed training or public derivative model. Do not infer it from this packet.
2. **Privacy:** an authorized reviewer inspects original and masked narratives privately, removes residual names, contacts, identifiers and re-identification context, records their identity, decision and rationale, and excludes unsafe records. Save a new immutable packet/version with hashes after corrections. Do not mark the existing automatically masked packet anonymous.
3. **Identity and language:** verify field meanings, readable complaint language, genuine institution/entity identity and dates from permitted evidence. Unknown identities remain unknown. Do not manufacture a canonical institution, translate silently or turn sentiment/support categories into relationships. Lack of context may make these records unsuitable for incident evaluation.
4. **Independent reference families:** a custodian establishes known event families before seeing model results, including hard negatives, different students/years/remedies, repeated boilerplate and missed positives. Mere identical wording is insufficient. Keep family and near-duplicate groups within one partition; freeze train/validation/calibration/test manifests before scoring.
5. **Blind judgments:** two distinct reviewers independently choose `same_incident`, `related_issue`, `unrelated` or `insufficient_evidence`, with source evidence and reasons. They must not see ranks/scores or each other's work. A third independent adjudicator resolves disagreements. Never force unknown identity into unrelated. Reuse `tests/research/public_evaluation.py::final_labels` validation and `tests/research/recall.py` for an independently established eligible reference set.
6. **Evaluation authorization:** only after the preceding gates can a separately frozen shadow evaluation be run. Returned-pair annotation alone cannot establish recall. Compare retrieval coverage, incident precision, abstentions, candidate truncation, latency and memory. Keep production MiniLM unchanged until a valid independent evaluation supports a candidate. Training and external sharing require their own recorded scope.

The current packet has **zero independent labels** and remains pending permission, manual privacy review and institution verification. Optional cross-encoder, alternative encoder and fine-tuning experiments are blocked for model selection until genuine ground truth exists. No public accuracy claim is justified by these support/sentiment labels.
